Same rules as list 4: 32x32 PNG, transparent, pixel art; moving = 4 frames stacked = 32x128.
File name EXACTLY as written, lower case. Upload to astra-art/UwU-Quest-Art-Pack/assets/unyieldingquests/textures/quest_art/
on GitHub (Add file > Upload files > Commit changes). Any quest you skip just keeps its item icon.
These quests currently show a plain item icon; the "shows now" line tells you what, so you can either
draw a nicer version of that item or something that fits the quest better.

TOTAL: 586 quests without a picture, in 29 chapters. (The Held Items page is left out on purpose - its 100 quests are single items and the item icons are right for them.)
This is a big list: do it in chunks and upload as you go; every upload gets built into the next jar. Do the chapters in any order.
