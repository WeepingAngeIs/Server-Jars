Unyielding 1.1.6 - live admin balance and music controls

Replace the older combined Unyielding JAR with Unyielding-1.1.6.jar on both the
server and clients. Stop the server and close Minecraft before replacing files.
Keep only one combined Unyielding JAR. Keep your external mod dependencies and
resource/song packs. Existing quest progress and configured settings are retained.

Open /quests settings as an operator (permission level 2).

Armor Balance: edit armor points, toughness and knockback percentages. Half and
normal presets are previews until Save. Changes apply to already-equipped armor
on loaded entities. Mod and item exceptions are editable too.

Raid IV odds: edit each of the seven tiers separately. Set the chances for one,
two or three guaranteed perfect IVs; the remainder gets none guaranteed. Edit
the ordinary IV cap or turn custom rolls off. Changes affect future raid rewards.
At an ordinary cap of 31, ordinary rolls may also produce perfect IVs.

Music mixer: change overall or individual track volume from 0% to 200%, including
Lavender Town. 100% preserves the existing audio level; 0% plays the track silently.
Changes reach connected clients and are saved with the server world for later joins.
Players retain their personal volume/mute controls. This integration targets
Reactive Music 1.3.5 NeoForge and the Cobblemon Unyielding songpack (32 tracks).
Everyone needs the updated Unyielding JAR and the matching songpack. New audio
files or changed biome/time triggers still require a songpack update.

Config values: shortcuts expose existing portal travel, Soul Tome and leaderboard
settings. Help describes when changes take effect; some update on the next use or
board refresh. Lowering capacity does not delete stored souls or active allies.

Existing defaults remain unchanged until an admin saves. Invalid values and stale
edits are rejected. Earlier spin editing and tutorial dismissal fixes are included.
