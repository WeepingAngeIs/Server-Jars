UNYIELDING QUESTS - ONE JAR  (v1.5.2)
=====================================
File: UnyieldingQuests-1.5.2.jar   (Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x)

INSTALL (click by click)
------------------------
1. Open the pack's mods folder:  C:\Users\tolov\curseforge\minecraft\Instances\Cobblemon Unyielding\mods
2. Delete UnyieldingQuests-1.5.1.jar (or any older UnyieldingQuests jar).
3. Delete these two too (they are the "FTB" buttons you saw at the top left):
     ftb-library-neoforge-2101.1.36.jar
     ftb-teams-neoforge-2101.1.11.jar
   In the CurseForge app also remove them from the pack's mod list (My Modpacks > Cobblemon
   Unyielding > Mods > find "FTB Library" and "FTB Teams" > the "..." menu > Remove), or the
   app puts them back on the next update. Your friend needs to leave them out of the next
   server pack / export as well.
4. Copy UnyieldingQuests-1.5.2.jar into mods/.
5. Same on the server: delete the old quests jar + the two FTB jars, add 1.5.2, restart.
Nothing else in the pack needs FTB: our jar does the quests, teams, homes/tpa/warps and
vein mining. Open Parties and Claims stays for claims.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.0  (your team's updated quest book + the changes you asked for)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The quest book in the jar is now built from ftbquests-server.zip (the team's updated pack),
with these edits on top. Player progress carries over (same quest ids).

 1. Welcome and Pack Tools: every quest on the page gives 25 CobbleDollars (relic coin icon,
    paid with /cobbledollars give, runs from the console). The "Reward references" quest keeps
    its own 100 CobbleDollars and seasoned snack - it is the example I copied from.
 2. Every Poke Snack reward anywhere in the book is now the SEASONED one
    (golden-apple bait effect / colour / ingredient, same as the reference).
 3. Legendary Expeditions (Kanto-Hoenn, Sinnoh-Kalos, Alola-Paldea): every quest gives
    1000 xp and 100 CobbleDollars.
 4. Type Mastery Trials: every quest gives 1000 xp and 100 CobbleDollars.
 5. REMOVED pages: Type Field Guide, Provisions and Berries, Evolution Essentials.
    Other quests that depended on quests from those pages no longer need them.
 6. Pokedex Adventures: rebuilt. The old quests and pictures are gone. Now:
      - 10 goals in a row: "Pokedex: 10% Caught" ... "Pokedex: 100% Caught". Each counts the
        Pokemon registered as CAUGHT against every Pokemon in the pack (addons included) and
        shows a progress bar. Rewards grow each step: Ultra Balls and Rare Candies at 10%,
        Ability Capsule at 30%, Master Ball + Ability Patch at 50%, up to 3 Master Balls,
        32 Rare Candies, 3 Enchanted Golden Apples, a Relic Coin Sack, 1000 CobbleDollars and
        2000 xp at 100%. (50% and 100% are the big hexagon quests.)
      - Moved in from "Exploration, Fossils and Utilities": the Pokedex, PokeNav, PokeFinder
        and FishingNav quests, all 4 Fossil Restoration quests and the 6 Fossil Collections
        by Region quests. Their Track Arrow / Poke Bait rewards are now seasoned Poke Snacks.
 7. Medicine and Recovery: rebuilt. 9 collection quests + 1 final:
      Every Potion (5), Every Status Cure (6), Back from the Brink (Revive + Max Revive),
      Every PP Item (Ether, Max Ether, Elixir, Max Elixir, PP Up, PP Max),
      Healing Berries (10), Pinch Berries (19), EV-Reset Berries (6), Type-Resist Berries (18),
      Garden Berries (17)  = all 70 berries.
      "The Complete Pharmacy" needs all nine and gives a Master Ball, 10 Rare Candies,
      Ability Capsule, 10 Full Restores, 5 Max Elixirs, 300 CobbleDollars and 1000 xp.
 8. Held Items and Battle Gear: "The Art of Held Items" is gone. Every held item (99 of them)
    is its own quest, packed close together in three columns under its old group name.
    Each gives 100 xp + 10 CobbleDollars (the first item of a group also keeps the group's old
    item reward). "Master Tactician" is the big gear at the bottom; it needs every held-item
    quest and gives 1000 xp + 500 CobbleDollars (relic coin sack icon) on top of its rewards.
 9. Dropped from 1.4.0: my Twilight Forest, Boss Hunts and Ars Nouveau chapters, because the
    team's updated book already has its own Twilight Forest, Cataclysm Boss Hunts, Bosses of
    Mass Destruction and Ars Nouveau pages. The other 7 chapters from 1.4.0 stay
    (Trainer Milestones, Raid Dens, Trainer League, Travel and Trade, Hobbies, Dungeons and
    Ruins, Biomes and Nature). Total now: 38 chapters, 967 quests.
10. The mod itself learned the FTB reward types the team's book uses: "command" rewards
    (/cobbledollars etc., {p} = player name, console or as-player), "loot" rewards (reward
    tables, weighted), "xp_levels", and a reward title (so command rewards show as
    "25 CobbleDollars" with the coin icon instead of a command line).
    Also new: Pokedex PERCENT goals for quests (dex_progress "caught_percent"/"seen_percent").
11. Art: Astra's pictures still match 65 of the team's quests; 53 pictures belong to quests
    that were removed (kept in the jar, harmless). New art request: Astra-Art-Sheet-4.xlsx.
12. Small fixes: the team's page headings ("Fossil Restoration", "Kanto", ...) now show on the
    pages (they were skipped before). A quest with lots of requirements says "finish A, B, C, D
    and 95 more quests on this page first" instead of listing all 99.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.4.0
-------------------
- Premier Ball button: a Premier Ball at the top-left of the inventory (survival AND creative)
  opens the quest book. A red dot on it means rewards are waiting. The Teams button sits next
  to it. Players can move or hide it: Mods > Unyielding Quests > Config > Client Settings >
  Inventory Buttons (Top-left corner / Next to the inventory / Hidden, plus X/Y position).
  EMI keeps its panels off the buttons.
- "Welcome to Cobblemon Adventures" is now "Welcome to Cobblemon Unyielding".
- LUCKY SPIN: every quest gives each player ONE bonus prize roll when they claim it
  (a "Lucky Spin" row in the quest's rewards, also included in "Claim all"). A wheel spins
  on screen and lands on the prize:
      50%  Basic prize   (Poke Balls, candies, potions, berries, iron, emeralds...)
      44%  Good prize    (golden apples, Master Ball (rare), netherite, evolution stones,
                          vitamins, Ability Capsule, mints, held items, totem, elytra (rare)...)
       3%  Paradox Pokemon
       2%  Mythical Pokemon
       1%  Legendary Pokemon
  Pokemon prizes have random stats, natures and abilities, level 20-35, and go to your party
  (PC if full). A Pokemon win is announced in chat to everyone.
  NOTE: Cobblemon 1.8.4 only has 2 Paradox Pokemon with models (Iron Leaves, Walking Wake),
  so the 3% always gives one of those two. Add more with extra_paradox if an addon adds them.
- DAILY LOGIN REWARD: one free Lucky Spin a few seconds after a player logs in, once per real
  day (Chicago time by default). Logging in 7 days in a row gives 2 extra spins that day.
  Daily odds are separate: 40% basic, 50% good, 5% Paradox, 3% Mythical, 2% Legendary.
  /daily shows a player's streak. Admins: /daily give <player>.
- 289 NEW QUESTS in 10 new chapters (1140 quests total):
    Twilight Forest (47)      - portal, every boss in order, side quests and loot
    Trainer Milestones (35)   - catch 10/50/100/250/500, win battles, evolve, level 100,
                                shinies, fishing, fossils, Pokedex counts, big achievements
    Raid Dens (15)            - join a raid, tiers 1-7, cheers, mega/dynamax/tera raids, shiny
    Trainer League (56)       - RCT trainers: Kanto, Sinnoh and Borrius badges, Elite Four,
                                champions, villain teams, Red and Blue
    Boss Hunts (21)           - Cataclysm overworld bosses, Night Lich, Void Blossom,
                                Wilden Chimera, Weeping Angels, Elder Guardian, Warden
    Ars Nouveau (28)          - spell books, machines, tamed helpers, rituals, gear
    Travel and Trade (15)     - Waystones, Lightman's coins, shops, bank, Cobble Merchant
    Hobbies and Gadgets (12)  - camera, instruments, Ribbit village, PokeNav, Poke Staff
    Dungeons and Ruins (31)   - YUNG's, Dungeons Arise, Towns and Towers, Explorify, more
    Biomes and Nature (29)    - 27 biomes from BOP, Biomes We've Gone and Nature's Spirit
  A new chapter group "World Exploration" holds the last four.
  Every item, boss, structure and biome id was checked against the exact mod versions in the
  pack. Quests finish by themselves (advancements, kills, visits, items in inventory).

CONFIG (server: config/unyieldingquests-common.toml, or Mods > Unyielding Quests > Config)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[lucky_spin]   enabled, chance_basic/good/paradox/mythical/legendary (weights; they are
               percentages when they add up to 100), pokemon_level_min/max,
               announce_pokemon_wins, basic_items / good_items (one per line:
               "item_id amount" or "item_id min-max", add x<weight> to make it rarer/commoner,
               e.g. "cobblemon:master_ball 1 x1"), legendary/mythical/paradox_labels,
               extra_legendary/mythical/paradox (add species), never_give (block species).
[daily_login]  enabled, the five chances, streak_bonus_every_days, streak_bonus_spins,
               timezone (America/Chicago).
Edits to the server file apply after /reload or a restart.

FOR ASTRA (next art)
--------------------
To make a picture move, stack its frames top to bottom in ONE PNG at the normal path
(32x128 = four 32x32 frames). The quest book plays them 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2 at 0.18 seconds each.
A normal square PNG stays a still picture. Quest pictures go in textures/quest_art/,
chapter icons in textures/chapter_icon/, same file names as before.

WHAT YOU GET
------------
1) Quests + Teams (same as 1.0.0): J key, 851 quests built in, team screen in inventory.

2) Essentials (replaces FTB Essentials). Commands:
   Homes/warps: /home /sethome /delhome /listhomes /warp /setwarp /delwarp /listwarps
   Teleport:    /spawn /playerspawn /back /tpa /tpahere /tpaccept /tpdeny /rtp /tpx /tpl /tp_offline /jump
   Utility:     /nick /rec /near /hat /heal /feed /fly /god /speed /extinguish /kickme /mute
   Workbenches: /anvil /crafting /smithing /stonecutter /enderchest /trashcan
   Admin:       /invsee /kit (create, delete, give, list)
   Config: config/unyieldingquests-essentials.toml
     - disabled_commands: list any command name to turn it off
     - cooldowns, home limits, /back limits, RTP range and blacklisted dimensions

3) Vein Mine (replaces FTB Ultimine)
   - HOLD the ` (backtick) key, then break a block. Matching blocks nearby break too.
   - Scroll the mouse wheel while holding to change the shape. An outline shows what will break.
   - Right-click with axe/hoe/shovel also works on neighbouring blocks.
   - Change the key in Options > Controls (category Unyielding).
   Config: config/unyieldingquests-ultimine.toml (max_blocks 64, hunger cost, tool rules)

4) Ranks: built in (already in 1.0.0). FTB Ranks is not needed.

NOT INCLUDED (compared with the FTB mods)
-----------------------------------------
  - Old FTB homes/warps are NOT copied over. Players set them again.
  - Ultimine "crystals" and the crop-harvest feature.
  - Teleport warm-up timers, and different home limits per rank.
  - /rtp is centred on world spawn.
  - /tpl and /tp_offline go to the player's last known spot.
  - /tpx <dimension> keeps your x/z.
  - Cross-dimension teleport was not confirmed in my test setup (same-dimension works).
    If /warp or /home to another dimension misbehaves on your server, tell me.
